📖 The book says —

Six decks · dealer stands on all 17s · blackjack pays 3:2 · double any two · double after split · split once. The shoe is one seeded shuffle — every finished hand replays bit-for-bit. Practice money: a public seed would show a sharp player the hole card, so sealed stakes await a hole-card commit/reveal protocol.

The in-game book isn't copied from a chart — it's derived: a dealer-outcome DP and player EV recursion in jack.js compute every hit/stand/double/split cell, and the tests prove the derivation lands on the canonical book, then play 200,000 hands with it (≈0.6% edge; guesswork donates multiples).
