How to play

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Cards 2–10 count their number, J/Q/K count 10, an ace counts 11 or 1 — whichever helps. An ace and a ten-card dealt together is a blackjack and pays 3 to 2.

  1. Bet. Tap chips to build your bet (it's kept between hands), then Deal.
  2. Play your hand. Hit takes a card. Stand keeps what you have. Double doubles the bet for exactly one more card. Split a pair into two hands (up to four), each with its own bet. Surrender gives up half the bet on your first two cards.
  3. The dealer plays by fixed rules: draws to 17 and stands on all 17s. Beat the dealer's total, or watch them bust, and you win even money.

The count

The shoe plays down to a cut card, so the cards remember. Turn on the count trainer in settings to see the Hi-Lo running count (2–6 are +1, 7–9 are 0, tens and aces −1) and the true count (running count per deck left). At each shuffle you'll be quizzed on the running count.

Drills

Drills deal only the tricky spots, one decision at a time, with no money. Get a spot right three times in a row (without the hint) and it's mastered 🏅; spots you haven't mastered come up more often. My leaks deals the spots you miss most.

The daily shoe

Everyone gets the same cards today — about 30 hands, at a table with a 100-chip limit. Start with 1,000, play it through, and share how cleanly you played it. Same cards for all — the only difference is how you play and what you bet. Par is what flat 25s played by the book make from the same cards. Scores run on the honour system: the shoe is public maths, not a secret.
